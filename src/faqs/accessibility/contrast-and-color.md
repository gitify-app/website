---
title: "How do I improve contrast and color readability?"
category: "Accessibility"
order: 1
---

Gitify offers high-contrast and color-blind friendly options, and it follows your operating system's increase-contrast setting.

- **Increase contrast** — in **Settings → Appearance**, turn on **Increase contrast**. Gitify also applies high-contrast colors automatically when your system's increase-contrast setting is on.
- **Color-blind themes** — in **Settings → Appearance → Theme**, choose a color-blind or Tritanopia variant.
- **Zoom** — use the zoom controls in **Settings → Appearance** to enlarge the interface.

The contrast and color-blind options are available with the **Classic** design. Glass follows the system increase-contrast setting by switching to solid surfaces.
