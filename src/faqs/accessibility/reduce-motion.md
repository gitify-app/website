---
title: "How do I make notification dismissal instant?"
category: "Accessibility"
order: 0
---

Turning on your operating system's Reduce Motion setting makes Gitify dismiss a notification immediately instead of sliding and fading the row out.

To enable Reduce Motion:

- **macOS** — open **System Settings → Accessibility → Display**, then turn on **Reduce motion**
- **Windows** — open **Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects**, then turn off **Animation effects**
- **Linux** — use your desktop environment's Reduce Motion or animation setting

This is useful on slow connections and when clearing several notifications quickly.

> The notification action still runs in the background. If it fails, the notification returns with the usual error feedback.
