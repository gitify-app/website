---
title: "How do I reduce transparency?"
category: "Accessibility"
order: 2
---

Gitify's Glass design follows your operating system's Reduce Transparency setting and switches to solid surfaces when it is on.

To enable Reduce Transparency:

- **macOS** — open **System Settings → Accessibility → Display**, then turn on **Reduce transparency**
- **Windows** — open **Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects**, then turn off **Transparency effects**
- **Linux** — use your desktop environment's Reduce Transparency setting

Gitify also switches Glass to solid surfaces when your system's increase-contrast setting is on.

> Reduce Transparency only changes the Glass design. The Classic design is already solid.
