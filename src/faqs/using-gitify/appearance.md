---
title: "How do I change the appearance of Gitify?"
category: "Using Gitify"
order: 2
---
You can change how Gitify looks from **Settings → Appearance**.

- **Design** — choose **Classic** or **Glass**
- **Theme** — choose **System** (follows your operating system), **Light**, or **Dark**. Extra themes depend on your design, including color-blind friendly and dimmed variants
- **Increase contrast** — use higher contrast for text and UI
- **Show status icon colors** — show colored status icons in the menu bar
- **Zoom** — zoom in, zoom out, or reset zoom for the whole app
- **Show account header** — show the account header in the sidebar
- **Show full notification title** — show full notification titles instead of truncating them
