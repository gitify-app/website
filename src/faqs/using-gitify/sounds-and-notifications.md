---
title: "How do I control sounds and system notifications?"
category: "Using Gitify"
order: 1
---
You can choose how Gitify alerts you to new notifications from **Settings → System**.

- **Show system notifications** — show native operating system notifications for new unread items
- **Play sound** — play a sound when new notifications arrive
- **Volume** — use the volume buttons to change the notification sound level, or reset it
